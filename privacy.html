CodeCraft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

CodeCraft is a game that teaches children to program. This page explains, in plain words, exactly what it collects, what it does not, and how to get rid of anything it holds.

The short version. You can play the entire game without an account, and if you do, nothing ever leaves your device. There is no advertising in CodeCraft, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. An account is optional, only available from age 13, and exists solely to sync your progress and let you publish levels.

If you play without an account

Most players never make one. In that case the game makes no network requests beyond downloading itself, and stores two things in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

WhatWhy
codecraft_save_v1 Your game — world, coins, robots, the programs you wrote, which lessons you finished. Never sent anywhere.
codecraft_age_v1 A single character, y or n: whether you were old enough for an account. See “Children” below.

Clearing your browser data removes both, and with them your progress.

If you make an account

Accounts are optional and only offered from age 13. Signing up is the only point at which CodeCraft collects personal information.

What is stored

DataWhyWho can see it
Email address and password To let you log back in. The password is stored only as a cryptographic hash — nobody, including us, can read it. You. Never shown to other players.
Your saved game So your progress follows you to another device. Progress, robots, programs, lesson history, your chosen nickname, and your block and report lists. Only you.
Challenges you publish The level itself plus the nickname you chose to sign it with. Everyone. This is the point of publishing.
Reports you send Which level, and which of five reasons you picked. Only you.
Players you hide So a blocked author stays hidden on your other devices too. Only you.
Your email is never attached to anything public. Published levels are signed with a nickname you pick yourself, never with your email or any part of it — the first half of an email address is very often a child's real name.

Where it is stored

On Supabase, our database provider, in their Frankfurt region (eu-central-1) inside the European Union. Row-level security is enforced on every table, so one player's account cannot read another's saved game.

What CodeCraft does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No social-media SDKs, no third-party trackers, no fingerprinting.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • No messaging between players — there is no chat in CodeCraft.

The app loads its font from its own server rather than from a font CDN, specifically so that opening the game does not reveal your IP address to a third party.

Who else is involved

Two service providers, and only in order to run the app:

  • GitHub Pages serves the game's files. Like any web server, it processes the IP address of anyone who loads a page.
  • Supabase stores accounts and saved games, as described above. It processes data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

Children

CodeCraft is made for children, and is built so that the youngest players have no personal data to protect in the first place.

When the game first opens it asks when you were born. It asks for a month and a year rather than “are you over 13?”, because a yes-or-no question simply teaches a child which answer unlocks the app. The date is not stored. It is turned into a single yes-or-no the moment it is entered, and only that is kept, on the device.

For anyone under 13, the game does not offer an account, does not offer publishing, and does not show the community list. Nothing is collected, so there is no parental consent to give — and nothing is taken away from the game itself: the world, the challenges, Tower, the level editor and market orders are all fully playable without an account.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us using the address below and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

In the app: tap delete account — it sits next to log out on the opening screen, and in the account box.

It removes, immediately and permanently: your login, your saved game, every challenge you published, and your report and block lists. It also clears the copy on that device. It cannot be undone, and no backup is kept from which it could be restored.

Playing without an account leaves nothing to delete on our side; clearing your browser data removes the local save.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR gives you the right to see the personal data held about you, to correct it, to have it erased, and to complain to your data protection authority. Deleting your account in the app already carries out an erasure in full. For anything else, write to us.

How long it is kept

Your account and saved game are kept until you delete them. There is no inactivity expiry, and no archive kept after deletion.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
